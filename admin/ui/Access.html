⏱ Grant access

Full console access — build and launch campaigns, not just view — for a fixed number of days. Internal @… addresses only. They sign in at this same URL; nothing needs sending to them beyond the link.
Access is checked on every request — not just at sign-in — so an expiry or a revoke takes effect on their very next click, not their next login. Nothing about this affects who can send mail under their own name: that is a separate, per-exec agent install (Operations tab), unaffected by console access.

✉ Sending access — time-boxed

This is different from console access above. Sending under someone's own name still needs them to authorize their own agent once — a real Google OAuth consent screen, Operations tab → agent Onboard link — there's no way around that step and no reason to want one; it's what guarantees this tool never holds their credentials. What you control here is how many days that authorization is allowed to keep sending. It stops on its own after that, with no further action needed from either of you.
