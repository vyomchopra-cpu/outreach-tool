✉ Send on behalf of someone

Ask a colleague to let campaigns go out under their name. They get one link, sign in as themselves, choose how many days, and approve. You never see their inbox and they never install anything.
Sending stops on its own when their window closes — nobody has to remember to switch it off. To go longer, ask again; there is deliberately no way to extend someone's window from this console.
Google shows a permission prompt before our page can explain itself, so send the whole message rather than a bare link — a CTO who meets an unexplained consent screen will close the tab.
